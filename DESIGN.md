# 🐾 Kennel Game

## The Big Idea
You work at a **pet kennel** — a place where people bring their pets to stay
while they're away. The kennel is YOUR work. You don't leave for work — you're
already there! All day long (and even at bedtime), you take care of every
single animal.

## The Animals
The kennel takes care of:
- 🐢 Turtles
- 🐹 Guinea pigs
- 🐹 Hamsters
- 🐰 Bunnies
- 🐱 Cats
- 🐶 Dogs

(Maybe more animals later!)

Every animal arrives with its own **name** — the owners name them, like
Cupcake the dog. Each cage has a **name tag on top**, so you always know who is
who — even if two dogs look exactly the same! And if two kittens look the same,
they get **colored collars** to tell them apart.

## The Sections
The kennel has a special section for each kind of animal:
a **turtle section**, a **guinea pig section**, a **hamster section**,
a **bunny section**, a **cat section**, and a **dog section**.
Every animal goes to its own section.

## Pets Checking In
People come **every day** to drop off their pets. **At least one animal arrives
every hour**, so there's always someone new coming through the door! Pets can
come and go in the daytime or the evening — but **not in the middle of the
night**.

When a pet arrives, you take it the right way:
- **Dogs** — you take the **leash**
- **Cats** — you take the **cage**
- **Guinea pigs, hamsters, and bunnies** — you just **gently pick them up**
- **A mom turtle** — she comes in a **box**, so you carry the box
- **Turtle eggs** — they come in a **basket**, so you carry the basket

Every pet **sleeps over at least 2 nights** — sometimes more. And here's the
best part: every animal **always comes back again someday**. If Cupcake goes
home, a few weeks later — ding! — Cupcake is back, over and over again. The
kennel is full of old friends.

## Feeding Time
You **feed** all the animals and give them **water**. The turtles need LOTS of
water — a turtle's home is a **little tank of water with a glass cover**, with a
**small sand island in the middle** where the turtles rest. The turtles and the
eggs **share the island together** — and there's plenty of space for everyone!

## Moms and Babies
Some pets come as a **family**: a mom with her babies!
- Turtles can come as a **mom with eggs** or a **mom with babies**
- **Kittens stay with their mom**, and **puppies stay with their mom**
- Inside the mom's cage there's a **little basket** just for the babies
- Some moms arrive **pregnant** — no eggs or babies yet, but they're GOING to
  have them!

Turtle eggs sit on the **sand island** and stay there until they hatch.
**Sometimes they hatch right there at the kennel** — then you take out the
shells! And sometimes they hatch at home instead — but don't worry, the family
always comes back to visit again.

When a mom is having her babies or laying her eggs, a little message pops up in
the **bottom left corner**: *"Cupcake is having babies!"*

When babies are born at the kennel, you get to use your **computer**: you type
a message like *"Cupcake had babies!"* and send it **with a picture of the
babies** to the owners. The owners write back with **names for the new
babies** — and then you put on their little collars and label their cages!

## The Playpens
The cats and kittens have a playpen, and the dogs and puppies have their own
**separate** playpen — so nobody gets in a fight! They play and play there.

## Potty Time 💩
Animals go to the bathroom, and it's your job to keep everything clean:
- In the playpens, you **pick it up with the scooper**
- **Cats** use **litter boxes**
- **Dogs** have to go **outside** — and a little message in the bottom left
  corner tells you WHO needs to go, by name:
  > *"Cupcake needs to go to the bathroom!"*

  …and then you take her out. Just in time!

## Night Time 🌙
At night, you tuck everyone in! You go to each cage and lay a **small sheet of
soft fabric** over each animal — or **wrap it around the eggs or babies** — to
keep them cozy while they fall asleep.

**You stay awake until every single animal is asleep.** And if you see an
animal sitting all by herself at night… that's the secret sign she's **going to
have babies** — maybe at the kennel, or maybe at home, and then she'll come
back next time WITH her babies!

Once everyone is asleep, you go to sleep too. But if an animal **wakes up in
the night**, it means one of these things:
- she's **having her babies**!
- she **needs to go to the bathroom**
- she had a **bad dream**
- she's **cold** — her fabric fell off and needs to be put back on

## Doing a Great Job ⭐
When you take really good care of a pet, the **owners give you new stuff** to
take even BETTER care of the animals next time — tools, a pet's favorite food,
toys, and more. The **owners also pay you money** for taking care of their
pets, and money can help you get the really good stuff. So every time an animal
comes back, their stay gets even better than the last one!
